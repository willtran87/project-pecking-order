# Rewarding Game Loop Resolution

This pass resolves the 25 intuitive, fun, and rewarding loop findings without creating another blocking screen. `GameplayPulseDirector.rewarding_loop` is a read-only presentation contract; simulation, Active Playbook, campaign, and office systems remain authoritative.

| # | Finding | Player-facing resolution |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | One-screen shift briefing | Three shape-distinct icons show Goal, Danger, and Reward beside the primary action. |
| 2 | World next-action beacon | The existing world outline and Take Me There target consume the single primary action. |
| 3 | Visible work pipeline | The physical trail is FILE → HEN → EGG → SORTER → CREDIT. |
| 4 | Short selected-hen panel | A hen now opens as Name, Specialty, Need, and Next Action; More reveals the full dossier. |
| 5 | Why feedback | Outcome receipts expose the filed cause and its first material effect. |
| 6 | Reward countdown | The shift goal rail shows actions remaining to the next payoff or Ready. |
| 7 | Decision cadence | The presentation contract targets one meaningful decision every 20–30 seconds. |
| 8 | Prediction choices | The authoritative prediction score remains visible through the Active Playbook. |
| 9 | Push your luck | Post-quota push-luck state is projected from the Playbook without hidden mutation. |
| 10 | Physical routing | Tray arming, folder landings, hen work, sorter travel, and credit remain world-linked. |
| 11 | Hen side objectives | Hen intent, optional personal goal, and proposal share one compact objective projection. |
| 12 | Distinct hen abilities | Contextual signature actions retain unique markers, camera focus, and audio families. |
| 13 | Relationship combos | Perchmate episodes and Team Lift retain both mechanical and physical payoff. |
| 14 | Incident staging | Physical cause, safe auto-pause, bounded choice, and recovery remain one incident sequence. |
| 15 | Transformative rewards | Choose-one drafts favor new rules, automation, recovery, teamwork, or visible office change. |
| 16 | Strategy identity | Strategy and furnishing loadout remain visible and stable between shifts. |
| 17 | Milestone spectacle | Golden and compound successes use world, camera, character, and audio beats. |
| 18 | Ten-second recap | The skippable Best Call / Close Call / What Changed highlight remains ten seconds. |
| 19 | Immediate recovery | Peck, Best Fit, and Care recover mistakes without deleting banked rewards. |
| 20 | Varied rematch | One-click rematch preserves the seed and changes exactly one rule. |
| 21 | Adaptive guidance | Contextual help never changes difficulty and retires after demonstrated success. |
| 22 | Play-introduced unlocks | Near, next-shift, and finale unlocks are staged through play instead of a front-loaded ledger. |
| 23 | Collections | Personal mastery and three fixed office display sockets make progress tangible. |
| 24 | Session hooks | Next shift, personal best, and challenge code provide an honest reason to return. |
| 25 | Comprehension playtests | Local instrumentation exists; real participant evidence is required and never fabricated. |

## Verification contract

- `tests/rewarding_game_loop_test.gd` verifies all 25 projections, the 3-card brief, 4-field dossier, 5-stage pipeline, 20–30 second cadence, causal feedback, recap, rematch, and honest research boundary.
- `tests/claim_routing_ui_test.gd` verifies the compact dossier is the default and that More restores all authoritative route, claimant, care, and profile controls.
- `web/tests/gameplay-pulse-audit.mjs` verifies the same version-7 contract in the exported browser build.

Real participant comprehension outcomes remain external release evidence. The product instruments the study but does not claim results that have not been observed.
