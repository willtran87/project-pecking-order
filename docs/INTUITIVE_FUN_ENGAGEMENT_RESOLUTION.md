game keeps one primary action visible, uses the office and hens as feedback, and
places exact numbers behind tooltips, Flockwatch, or report details. Existing
systems are reused when they already satisfy an item; duplicating them would
make the game less intuitive.

## Honest validation boundary

Automated tests establish wiring, determinism, layout, accessibility semantics,
and cause/effect. They do not prove that a human finds the result fun or
addictive. Moderated first-session comprehension and enjoyment sessions remain
the appropriate empirical follow-up, and the privacy-local playtest receipt
exists for that purpose without transmitting player data.
