# Intuitive and Rewarding Gameplay Completion

This release resolves the approved 33-item gameplay brief at a professional standard while preserving one simulation authority and one concise default interface. It adds no parallel economy, roster, queue, or blocking dashboard.

## Player-facing result

The default read is now `NOW → WHY → REWARD`, capped at six words per beat. Actions preview their world target and physical path; deeper gain, cost, and risk details remain available on demand. The first minute is a playable, skippable `PLAN → ROUTE → HELP → REWARD` sequence, and the shift closes through a decisive clutch finish, ten-second review, and one concrete next-shift promise.

Relationships now develop into a real management choice. A Good Perch pair can use its specialty-specific Team Lift; at Clutchmates 75 the pair permanently chooses Precision Duet or Recovery Pact. Precision adds career growth and attention, while Recovery adds morale and strain relief. Both hens remember the choice across checkpoints.

Rivals now remember the player's prior responses across shifts. Defending, counter-pushing, or backing the flock writes a bounded campaign record and changes the rival's visible personality and next intent—without hidden catch-up rules. Hero files also gain a prepared physical slot, first-egg arrival cue, review/future callback, persistent archive, and immediate-repeat protection.

## Evidence boundary

Automated tests prove state ownership, persistence, exact tradeoffs, projection shape, responsive presentation, and browser delivery. They cannot prove that five unaided people understand or enjoy the game. The production diagnostic therefore remains `AWAITING REAL PARTICIPANTS`, `results_complete: false`, and `never_fabricate: true` until five consented sessions satisfy [FIRST_SHIFT_PLAYTEST_CARD.md](FIRST_SHIFT_PLAYTEST_CARD.md).
