# Engagement Advancement Resolution

This release resolves the fourteen engagement findings as one bounded extension of the existing management loop. It adds no currency, permanent HUD panel, parallel worker authority, or daily chore.

## Player-facing rhythm

The layer preserves one readable cadence:

1. Pick one plan and see its forecast.
2. Route physical files and respond to one contextual opportunity at a time.
3. Let a hen propose, rescue a mistake, or enjoy one optional office beat.
4. Reconcile the result, compare the personal best, and see one named hen story.
5. Carry the build identity or challenge code into the next attempt.

## Reliability boundaries

- `active_playbook` remains same-day, strictly validated save authority. Older checkpoints restore with safe defaults.
- Worker assignments, relationships, career state, scenario seed, and workday reports remain the durable authorities.
- Practice and challenge-code copy remain presentation-only and do not alter the simulation.
- All new one-shot state resets at day rollover, preventing duplicate proposals, rescues, toys, or episodes.
- The three display sockets reuse fixed world geometry; no free-placement transform is saved or trusted.

## Human comprehension protocol

Recruit five participants unfamiliar with the project. Give no verbal or written instruction beyond launching a fresh game. Observe whether each participant can choose a plan, route one file, recover one mistake, and explain one result. The release target is at least four of five completing all four tasks unaided. Record time-to-first-plan, time-to-first-delivery, rescue discovery, and the participant's own cause-and-effect explanation.

This document defines the study and the build emits its instrumentation contract. It does not claim that human sessions have occurred.
