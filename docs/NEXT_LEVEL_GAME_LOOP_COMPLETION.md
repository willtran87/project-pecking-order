# Next-Level Game Loop Completion

This pass resolves the 25 requested improvements at point of play without adding a competing HUD, economy, or command path. `DepartmentSimulation` remains the sole gameplay writer; the completion layer is an audited, read-only projection.

## Immediate clarity

- One armed file becomes the sole primary action: **FILE → HEN → RESULT**.
- Every valid hen now carries a color-independent fit shape: **star = BEST**, **check = SAFE**, **triangle = RISKY**.
- Each target exposes the same compact forecast contract: pace, shell risk, and reward behavior.
- Invalid drops return the file, while click, keyboard, touch, and undo paths remain available.
- The world reaction chain remains physical and visible: **FILE → HEN → EGG → SORTER → CREDIT**.
- Teaching follows **SHOW → TRY → EXPLAIN ON DEMAND**, with no mandatory prose block.

## Reward and mastery

- The first prepared file targets a meaningful reward inside 30 seconds.
- Combo recipes expose the next step, preserve recoverable breaks, and feed the existing push-your-luck routing system.
- Combo completion uses anticipation, landing, flock reaction, and reward-settling beats, with equivalent reduced-motion feedback.
- Short permanent mastery dockets have no expiry or miss penalty.
- Same-seed, one-rule rematches keep banked progress safe and make experimentation immediate.

## Strategy, character, and progression

- Each shift carries one concise identity, one visible objective, and one mid-shift strategy pivot with multiple valid answers.
- Chicken specializations are mechanically distinct and surfaced at the actual drop target.
- Personal objectives, career callbacks, partnerships, shared reactions, and persistent flock outcomes reinforce individual hens.
- Rival intent, personality, and remembered responses remain visible rather than driven by hidden counters.
- Transformative upgrades change verbs and the physical office; progression lands in visible collection sockets.
- Automation removes solved chores while leaving exceptions under manual control.

## Recovery and evidence

- Setbacks convert into actionable recovery arcs without deleting banked rewards.
- The ten-second recap shows what worked, the close call, an alternate path, and the next shift.
- Information density adapts to hesitation without changing difficulty.
- Positive, safe, and risky outcomes use consistent shape, motion, and sound families that survive muted audio and reduced motion.
- The first-shift observation protocol is instrumented, but its human evidence remains honestly marked **AWAITING REAL PARTICIPANTS** until at least five real participants are observed.

## Release boundary

Automated tests verify the exact 25 unique items, the BEST / SAFE / RISKY preview schema, simulation non-mutation during selection, marker cleanup after dispatch, browser projection, responsive layouts, and release packaging. Automated checks do not substitute for the pending real-person usability study.
