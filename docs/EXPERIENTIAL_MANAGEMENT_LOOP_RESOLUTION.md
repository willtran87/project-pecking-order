## Authority and safety contracts

- `DepartmentSimulation` is the sole authority for the new intervention, including exact costs, effects, one-use enforcement, checkpoint validation, and shift reset.
- `GameplayPulseDirector` version 11 is read-only. Its `experiential_management_loop` maps all twenty surfaces and explicitly adds no default panel.
- Cause replay is presentation-only. It emits no dispatch landing or reward signal, performs no save, changes no simulation state, and frees its temporary folder after the animation.
- Existing schema-v30 saves remain compatible because the new nested intervention field is optional on restore and receives the empty default on the next shift.
- Automated tests prove deterministic mechanics and presentation contracts. They do not claim real-player comprehension, fun, or retention results.
