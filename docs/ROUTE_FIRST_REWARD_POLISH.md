# Route-first reward polish

This pass addresses the latest ten-point review through visible gameplay changes, not another completion-score projection.

1. **One opening goal.** Fresh careers show one first-egg target, followed by the first-clutch and shift goals. Skipping induction restores the normal quota display.
2. **A shorter lesson.** The featured hen's specialty route starts automatic production. Her first real collected egg opens reinvestment without requiring a check-in or Priority Peck. Existing saves retain their original lesson; no paid action or bonus is fabricated.
3. **Predictable controls.** The induction action uses a stable minimum footprint and direct labels. Personnel actions show FREE or the exact Feed Fund price before purchase.
4. **Readable attention.** Overview hen markers share three categories: working, opportunity, and help needed. The selected file retains the underlying cause and available action; urgent care/deadlines outrank routine progress.
5. **Decision feedback.** Existing fit, timed-assist, recovery, and equipment-to-egg feedback remain connected to real outcomes. Choosing a strategy adds a named-hen reaction, a bounded office pulse, and a concise consequence receipt.
6. **Visible strategy tradeoffs.** Fast, Safe, and Flock choices disclose their existing pace, shell-risk, strain, and Feed Fund effects in the menu. This is disclosure of actual simulation effects, not a new balance layer.
7. **Breathing room.** First collection has a two-second presentation pause before reinvestment. New ambient chatter can wait up to three seconds after major actions; important character decisions remain eligible. Pending rewards survive another open management surface without replacing it.
8. **Understandable recovery.** Selected needs lead to the existing Support/file controls, with cause details and visible action costs. Already-filed rewards are not erased by a warning; choosing a paid recovery still spends its disclosed cost.
9. **A relevant next shift.** Review proposes an optional improvement based on cracks, overdue work, missed quota, or a successful rested flock. It does not automatically buy a strategy or invent a challenge payout.
10. **Real-player validation: pending.** The five-person study card now asks whether players understand automatic work, recognize attention categories, notice tradeoffs, and voluntarily continue. Actual sessions must be supplied; automated checks cannot prove fun, comprehension, or retention.

## Verification

The native route-first regression exercises real routing, production, collection, the reinvestment offer, save normalization, and deferred presentation. Legacy keyboard/check-in/Peck tests remain separate. Dialogue tests cover deferred ambient chatter, urgent bypass, resumption, and session reset. Browser audits use actual controls and capture the production export at high-resolution, normal, and compact sizes.
