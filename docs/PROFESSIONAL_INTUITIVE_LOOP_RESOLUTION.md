# Professional Intuitive Loop Resolution

This pass resolves the twenty high-impact clarity and engagement findings as one presentation system. The simulation, campaign, routing, and Active Playbook remain authoritative; `GameplayPulseDirector.professional_loop` is a read-only projection used by the office HUD, browser diagnostics, and release tests.

| # | Finding | Shipped resolution |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Single-focus HUD | The current action owns the strongest contrast while secondary loop, reward, and ledger chrome recedes. Details remain available on demand. |
| 2 | World-first routing | Arm a physical tray, then select the world hen. Mouse, touch, keyboard, camera focus, outline, undo, and dossier-on-demand share the same route authority. |
| 3 | Three-icon consequence preview | Benefit, cost, and risk appear as distinct egg, coin-stack, and shield shapes beside the primary action. Exact values remain in hover, focus, and accessible copy. |
| 4 | Physical production journey | Every job follows the visible five-beat trail: FILE → HEN → EGG → SORTER → CREDIT. |
| 5 | Immediate action receipts | Consequences use world-directed motion, a maximum of three deltas, semantic icons, sound, and accessible text. |
| 6 | Visible next payoff | The Active Playbook exposes exact progress, target, remaining actions, and ready state for the next payoff. |
| 7 | Strong strategy previews | Fast, Safe, and Flock expose gain, cost, risk, recommendation, and their visible office effect before filing. |
| 8 | Contextual signature button | When a hen power is ready, the HUD names the actual move—such as `SHARE CREDIT [Q]`—instead of a generic Signature label. Q opens the playbook and never files a choice. |
| 9 | Signature spectacle | Signature actions have anticipation, camera focus, a unique worker beat, impact receipt, sound family, and settle phase. |
| 10 | Route-combo choreography | Two-step recipes expose progress, next step, world markers, and the resulting effect. |
| 11 | Better shift pacing | Six readable stages—Calm, Flow, Pressure, Incident, Final Push, Celebrate—control intensity and reserve the strongest pulse for urgent beats. |
| 12 | Interactive incidents | Physical warning precedes a safely paused choice; consequences and recovery follow the authoritative decision. |
| 13 | Transformative reward drafts | Up to three options are compared and exactly one is chosen; rewards change a rule or visible world state. |
| 14 | Strategy visibly changes office | Strategy identity and furnishing loadout persist between shifts and drive office atmosphere rather than rebuilding arbitrary décor. |
| 15 | Chicken-driven opportunities | Hen intent and authored optional proposals enter the Active Playbook as worker-owned opportunities. |
| 16 | Relationship payoffs | Relationship episodes connect paired world choreography to authoritative teamwork effects. |
| 17 | Expressive recovery | Setbacks offer a fail-forward move, safe banked rewards, hen reactions, and organic breakroom recovery props. |
| 18 | Ten-second shift highlight | The skippable three-beat recap presents Best Call, Close Call, and What Changed in ten seconds, with an instant reduced-motion alternative. |
| 19 | One-click rematch variations | Remix preserves the seed and results while changing exactly one disclosed rule. |
| 20 | Real comprehension playtests | Local instrumentation defines four participant tasks and four measures. The build explicitly requires real participants and never fabricates usability evidence. |

## Release contracts

- Native: `tests/professional_intuitive_loop_test.gd`
- Browser: `web/tests/gameplay-pulse-audit.mjs`
- Release gate: `tools/verify_beta_release.ps1`
- Projection version: `6`

Automated checks prove the system is wired and internally consistent. Human comprehension results remain an external evidence step: a real participant must name the goal, route a file, explain the result, and recover one mistake before any playtest outcome can be claimed.
