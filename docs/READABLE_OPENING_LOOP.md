# Readable opening-loop pass

This pass changes visible controls and feedback rather than adding another feature-completion score. Automated checks establish behavior, not enjoyment.

## Shipped presentation changes

- Day one's first three laid eggs fill three physical slots. The full shift quota remains available in the tooltip; the normal quota display returns after the opening clutch.
- The induction card directly routes the featured hen, files her check-in, starts work, and offers the available Priority Peck. Existing keyboard controls and authoritative production handlers remain in use.
- Short action labels and a file → hen → egg → reward cue replace competing early instructions. Optional Playbook priorities are postponed until three eggs; emergency help remains available.
- The focused hen distinguishes working, specialty fit, egg delivery, care needs, and recovery. Routine production remains automatic.
- Routing Flow discloses its next actual milestone and reward. Clean-shell credit has a separate explicit label. When quota is met, guidance exposes the existing bank/chase tradeoff.
- Purchased equipment keeps its physical desk changes and marks the next newly laid egg, then explains the upgrade when that specific egg reaches collection. The explanation does not promise that probabilistic safety upgrades guarantee a clean egg.
- Shift-result captions now match their actual values. Operating net includes contract breach costs. A named hen's contribution leads the personal recap, with detailed accounting folded away.

## Existing systems retained

The production journey, specialty speed/safety benefits, optional interventions, shift pacing, authored personality behavior, recoverable setbacks, replay choices, and next-shift opportunities remain connected to their existing simulation authorities. This pass simplifies their presentation; it does not claim they are newly implemented.

## Human validation outstanding

The fifteenth recommendation requires five actual new players. Use `FIRST_SHIFT_PLAYTEST_CARD.md` and the existing consent-based evidence workflow. No participant results or claims of increased fun/retention are fabricated. Comprehension and enjoyment remain hypotheses until those sessions are recorded.
