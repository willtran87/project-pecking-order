# Compelling Game Loop Resolution

This pass resolves the 30 approved clarity, fun, mastery, character, reward, and accessibility findings without adding another blocking screen. `GameplayPulseDirector.compelling_loop` is a read-only projection; simulation, routing, Active Playbook, campaign, audio, and office systems remain authoritative.

| 2 | Before/after previews | Existing world-safe action previews now expose current target plus projected gain, cost, risk, and uncertainty without filing. |
| 3 | Three-part action feedback | Every important action is projected across world trail, hen reaction, and receipt/audio channels. |
| 4 | Strong shift identities | Strategy, rhythm stage, challenge rule, next reward, and physical loadout form one shift identity. |
