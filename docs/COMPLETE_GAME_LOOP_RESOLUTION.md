# Complete game-loop resolution

This pass turns the latest 24-item intuitiveness and engagement review into one
compact presentation layer. It adds no currency, idle accrual, FOMO timer, or
mandatory maintenance task. `DepartmentSimulation` and the Active Playbook
remain the only gameplay authorities.

## The playable rhythm

- The fresh-player funnel now exposes a skippable 90-second micro-shift:
  `PLAN → ROUTE → REACT → REWARD`. Completed labels retire while their four
  learned icons remain.
- Every live shift now has a derived six-stage rhythm: calm, flow, pressure,
  incident, final push, and celebration. Only an incident or final push receives
  the strongest pulse.
- Holding `H` pauses the live clock and opens four glance chips: what to do,
  where to do it, what to watch, and what it earns. Releasing `H` closes the
  strip and safely restores the prior speed.

## Cause, character, and payoff

- Decision and Active Playbook receipts animate from the filed choice toward
  the affected UI value or named hen. Worker consequences land on the chicken
  with the largest authoritative morale/stress change and trigger a short bark.
- A three-beat episode projection combines the focused hen's remembered move,
  named perchmate relationship, and current pressure. Existing authored
  dialogue, careers, case memories, and relationship saves remain authoritative.
- The end-of-shift review uses `WHAT WORKED`, `CLOSE CALL`, and `WHAT CHANGED`
  card semantics. Exact accounting stays behind the existing Details fold.
- The review adds compact one-click replay and remix icons: replay re-stages the
  filed hen highlight without changing state; remix cycles a preview-only Fast,
  Safe, or Flock idea before Continue.

## Findings covered

The stable `complete_game_loop` diagnostic maps all 24 findings to real game
surfaces: first-shift onboarding, semantic action icons, cause/effect trails,
tactile feedback, escalating rhythm, transformative strategy identity, chicken
memory, emergent stories, recovery, reward cadence, opening plans, dilemma
callbacks, alternative victory framing, rival counterplay, office legacy,
tactical unlocks, replay flow, next-shift hooks, hold-to-explain, three-card
reports, retiring labels, contextual controls, urgency hierarchy, and success
anticipation.
