## Boundaries

- No change to economy, egg production rates, staffing identity, office furnishing placement or shift transitions.
- Optional goal data remains validated primitive version-1 data. Older goals continue to restore; welfare adds a bounded 0–100 target. Missing closing welfare cannot count as success.
- Browsing does not replace an accepted goal. There is no goal cash reward or failure penalty.
- The egg-flight clock changes presentation timing only. Native deterministic stepping and the existing exactly-once delivery authority are retained.
- Browser fixtures verify layout and controls, not human enjoyment. The five-person study and normal-device first-payoff target remain unvalidated until recorded on the deployed candidate.
