# Economic gameplay acceptance record

This record resolves the supplied general economy checklist against the actual
design of *Pecking Order*. “Shipped” means the requirement is present in the
authored claims-bureau game. “Scoped out” means the conditional suggestion would
conflict with that game rather than improve it. “External sign-off” identifies
checks that software automation cannot honestly replace.

The player runs Farm Mutual’s Egg Yield Bureau. Claims are the work supply,
seated peckwork is production, graded eggs are completed services, and Farmer
Review settles revenue and obligations. This is not a commodity-trading game:
Farm Mutual sets disclosed binder terms, and the player chooses which book,
rider, service route, staffing plan, capital project, and workplace policy to
accept.

## Core Economic Gameplay

1. **Engaging loop — shipped.** Inspect demand, route named hens, supervise the
   shift, grade/collect completed claims, settle exact costs, then reinvest.
2. **Meaningful economic decisions — shipped.** Production, contract selection,
   investment, staffing, automation, facilities, procurement, and allocation are
   all player-controlled; generic retail price-setting is intentionally replaced
   by negotiated insurance binders.
3. **Interconnected systems — shipped.** Staffing changes payroll, feed demand,
   capacity, throughput, welfare, Treasury exposure, and future contract access.
4. **Distinct resources — shipped.** Feed Fund, protected reserve, Treasury
   headroom, feed stock, Farm Mutual standing, live-file capacity, welfare,
   compliance, favor, and exposure have different sources and gates.
5. **Source/use/limit/value — shipped.** Capital’s Economic Briefing files those
   four explanations for every primary economic resource.
6. **Generation/consumption balance — verified.** Authored balance profiles and
   bounded capacity prevent permanently useless resources or hard scarcity.
7. **Short/long tradeoffs — shipped.** Overtime, denial, surveillance, bulk feed,
   care, training, hiring, credit, contracts, and construction exchange immediate
   output for later liabilities or resilience.
8. **Different strategies — shipped.** Throughput, quality, welfare, contract,
   and capacity routes are documented in Capital with lever, upside, and risk.
9. **No universal dominant strategy — verified.** Deterministic campaign balance
   profiles test each route and recovery play.
10. **Planning over clicking — shipped.** Simulation work is automatic after
    routing; decisions, not click frequency, determine results.
11. **No decisionless waits — shipped.** Pause/1x/3x/10x, events, routing,
    dossiers, interventions, and fast-forward keep the next decision available.

## Supply, Demand, and Markets

1. **Responsive supply and demand — shipped.** Deterministic seasonal lane demand
   combines with accepted binders, storage, staffing, and intake capacity.
2. **Learnable price movement — shipped.** The filed season changes binder value,
   breach exposure, and the feed spot quote.
3. **Explain changes — shipped.** Market cards and Economic Briefing show current
   season, lead lane, percentage movement, feed quote, next turn, and rationale.
4. **Trends/shortages/preferences — shipped where appropriate.** Five-close
   margin trend, feed shortage/surplus, and lane demand are present. Generic
   competitors and geographic regions are scoped out because Farm Mutual is the
   authored client and the bureau is one office.
5. **Opportunity, not punishment — shipped.** Every season raises some lane or
   makes a procurement/contract hedge valuable.
6. **Anticipation — shipped.** Next market window, lead lane, and spot quote are
   forecast before commitment.
7. **Controlled uncertainty — shipped.** Seeded claim variation and authored
   incidents are reproducible; the market calendar itself cannot be rerolled.
8. **No stagnant market — shipped.** Three-day seasons rotate lane demand and
   Winter raises all demand while capacity remains bounded.
9. **Resource sinks — shipped.** Feed, wages, maintenance, severance, care,
   training, procurement spoilage, debt interest, facilities, and breach costs.
10. **No runaway inflation — verified.** Integer-cent pricing, authored caps,
    recurring obligations, bounded campaign scale, and no compounding resale.

## Production and Operations

1. **Clear connected chain — shipped.** File intake → routing → seated peckwork →
   egg → grading/rework → dispatch → Farmer Review.
2. **Increasing service value — shipped.** Sound/golden quality, settlement,
   specialist handling, contract riders, and Service Coop standing increase
   completed-claim value; “raw materials” are service files, not commodities.
3. **Visible bottlenecks — shipped.** Capital ranks Treasury, feed, archive,
   workflow debt, and break-even constraints with exact causes.
4. **Diagnostic tools — shipped.** Live/capacity/rejected counts, feed stock and
   coverage, idle worker states, rework/overdue files, margins, and close history.
5. **Throughput improvements — shipped.** Staffing, routing, managers, facilities,
   logistics, training, and bounded AUTO support.
6. **Capacity planning — shipped.** Desks, records, live-file storage, service
   limits, management span, and feed bins are separate constraints.
7. **Strategic automation — shipped.** AUTO reduces routing work but retains
   staffing, seated work, shell risk, compliance exposure, and player policy.
8. **Priorities/routing/policies — shipped.** Manual/AUTO trays, lane routing,
   Priority Peck, manager posture, directives, riders, and doctrines.
9. **Expansion complexity — shipped.** Every new room adds capability, capital
   cost, upkeep/payroll, gates, risks, and visible construction.
10. **No solved-loop micromanagement — shipped.** AUTO and time controls handle
    routine flow; consequential exceptions remain manual.

## Costs, Revenue, and Profitability

1. **Financial categories — shipped.** Secured income, variable feed, hen and
   supervisor payroll, fixed facility/campus/portfolio costs, investment,
   protected reserves, principal, interest, arrears, and margin are distinct.
   Taxes are scoped out because they do not serve the five-shift satire.
2. **Transparent profit — shipped.** Economic Briefing shows secured income minus
   full filed operating cost and the exact remaining break-even gap.
3. **Income/expense breakdown — shipped.** Farmer Review and Capital expose exact
   categories and Treasury receipts.
4. **Comparison — shipped where relevant.** Contract terms, supplier lots,
   facilities, staffing, strategies, and departments expose current/next deltas.
   Region comparison is scoped out.
5. **Pricing effects — adapted and shipped.** The bureau accepts/negotiates
   binder and rider terms that alter volume, standing, reserve, welfare gates,
   deadlines, and margin. Arbitrary retail price sliders are scoped out.
6. **Diminishing returns — shipped.** Facility tiers, standing gates, finite
   management actions, storage, workload, and recurring costs bound scaling.
7. **Efficiency retains constraints — shipped.** Automation and upgrades never
   create files, bypass seated work, erase payroll/feed, or avoid grading.
8. **Plannable investments — shipped.** Previews show capital, added daily cost,
   capacity/effect, prerequisites, and protected-fund result.
9. **Financial risks — shipped.** Maintenance, spoilage, interest, seasonal
   pricing, wage arrears, breach reserves, and overexpansion.
10. **Recovery without immunity — shipped.** Standard book, Treasury bridge,
    idle-perch release, feed hedge, retriable probation, and restructuring all
    carry explicit tradeoffs.

## Progression and Growth

1. **Scale/complexity/influence — shipped.** One floor grows into a service and
   governance campus with a larger flock, manager hierarchy, standing, and Board
   Book consequences.
2. **Gradual introduction — shipped.** First Clutch and five probation shifts
   stage routing, quality, finance, labor, contracts, facilities, and governance.
3. **Upgrades unlock strategies — shipped.** Rooms unlock care, credentials,
   negotiation, procurement, dispatch, relations, automation, and new capacity.
4. **Milestones — shipped.** First clutch, targets, facilities, standing tiers,
   contracts, promotions, campus parcels, probation result, and Senior file.
5. **Transformative/visible upgrades — shipped.** Purchases build cumulative
   modeled rooms, props, workers, routes, signs, and service hardware.
6. **Build identity — shipped.** Challenge contract, doctrine, annual strategy,
   routing style, manager posture, riders, and competing capital commitments
   replace a generic technology tree.
7. **Competing choices — shipped.** Protected cash, upkeep, capacity, time, and
   irreversible policy consequences prevent frictionless purchasing.
8. **Earlier systems remain relevant — shipped.** Hens, routing, feed, quality,
   standing, and claims remain inputs to every later system.
9. **No grind walls — verified.** Probation is five authored shifts; retries and
   guidance do not demand idle accumulation.
10. **Deep late game — shipped.** Optional Senior Roost adds annual strategy,
    precedents, portfolio, settlement, governance, and recovery—not bigger-only
    multipliers.

## Difficulty and Economic Pressure

1. **Tradeoff-driven difficulty — shipped.** Later books combine tighter margin,
   clauses, labor, debt, demand, and governance consequences. Generic AI
   competitors are scoped out.
2. **Paced challenges — shipped.** Systems unlock by shift/day and prerequisites.
3. **Adaptation without constant game-over — shipped.** Reserve protection,
   warnings, recovery files, pauses, standard book, and retriable probation.
4. **Early warnings — shipped.** Cash/reserve/free fund, break-even, liabilities,
   feed shortage, capacity pressure, arrears, deadlines, and breach exposure.
5. **Actionable causes — shipped.** The primary bottleneck always includes WHY
   and ACT text sourced from authoritative state.
6. **Multiple recoveries — shipped.** Credit, downsizing, changed production,
   standard/repriced contract book, procurement hedge, and restructuring.
7. **No hidden bankruptcy — verified.** Spending preflights protect obligations;
   deterministic receipts reconcile every cent.
8. **Fair scaling — shipped where relevant.** Market and operating complexity
   advance on authored days with disclosed requirements; competitors are N/A.
9. **Adjustable difficulty — shipped.** Challenge contracts alter targets,
   welfare/compliance/favor pressure, shell conditions, and recovery context;
   starting-resource or competitor sliders are outside the authored campaign.

3. **Charts/forecasts/tooltips/filters/summaries — shipped where useful.** A

4. **What changed and why — shipped.** Receipts, status history, close ledgers,
   market explanation, and bottleneck reason.
5. **Rates/inventory/margin/capacity/time — shipped.** Peck pace, ration demand,

6. **Loss/irreversibility warnings — shipped.** Contracts, resolutions, personnel
   actions, doctrine, facilities, and backup import show exact consequences.
7. **Upgrade preview — shipped.** Cost, daily obligation, prerequisites, effects,
   capacity, and projected spendable fund.
8. **Consistent notation — verified.** Currency is integer cents displayed as
   dollars; percent, basis points, scoops, files, hens, shifts, and minutes are
   explicitly labeled.
9. **Time controls — shipped.** Pause, 1x, 3x, and 10x.
10. **Advanced detail optional — shipped.** Today/Flock are primary; Capital,
    Ops, Records, dossiers, and disclosures provide deeper statistics.

## Controls and Interface

1. **Fast actions — shipped.** Contextual controls and persistent Flockwatch pages
   cover purchasing, staffing, facilities, routing, and reorganization.
2. **Low click/confirmation burden — shipped.** Confirmations are reserved for
   consequential or irreversible filings.
3. **Bulk/repeat/automation — adapted and shipped.** Procurement uses meaningful
   authored lots instead of click-grind quantity spinners; AUTO, speed controls,
   batch shift simulation, and repeatable routing remove routine work.
4. **Relevant placement — shipped.** Actions live in Today, Flock, Ops, Capital,
   Records, the focused dossier, or current modal context.
5. **Clean responsive dashboards — verified.** Progressive pages, adaptive HUD,
   compact Today, disclosures, and responsive wrapper are regression-tested.
6. **Consistent semantic language — shipped.** Teal/gold/warning/danger treatment
   is paired with words, values, and icons rather than color alone.
7. **Self-explaining buttons — shipped.** Cost, effect, held/ready state,
   prerequisite, and reason remain visible.
8. **Preserved choices — shipped where applicable.** Page/disclosure/filter state
   survives refreshes within the management session; durable preferences,
   controls, audio, accessibility, difficulty, and campaign state are saved.
9. **Resolution/input coverage — verified.** Desktop, portrait, landscape,
   keyboard, mouse, controller, and touch contracts have automated coverage.
10. **Configuration — shipped.** Remapping, independent audio, presentation
    modes, text scale, simulation speed, reduced motion, color vision, and
    challenge contract.

## Onboarding and Learning

1. **Learning through decisions — shipped.** First Clutch teaches by inspecting,
   routing, working, grading, collecting, and reinvesting.
2. **One major system at a time — shipped.** Progressive unlock schedule.
3. **Production/expense/demand/profit relationship — shipped.** First review and
   Capital briefing connect secured claim work to exact operating margin.
4. **Safe experimentation — shipped.** Early claims, pause, save checkpoints,
   standard-book fallback, recovery, and retriable probation.
5. **Contextual first-use guidance — shipped.** Page discovery, tutorial prompts,
   tooltips, blocked reasons, and inspection hints.
6. **Plain terminology — shipped.** Satirical label is paired with practical
   description, exact value, and units.
7. **Skip/revisit/disable — shipped.** First Clutch can be skipped; handbook,
   disclosures, dossiers, and guidance can be revisited; sensory guidance can be
   reduced.
8. **Durable early habits — shipped.** Inspect, route for fit, protect shells,
   watch strain/reserves, and compare tradeoffs stay relevant.
9. **What and why — shipped.** Buttons state effects while forecasts and dossiers
   explain strategic reasons.

3. **Decision events — shipped.** Incidents, petitions, claimants, contracts,

4. **Future consequences — shipped.** Appeals, trust, standing, clauses, labor
   cases, doctrine, precedents, and annual strategy persist.

## Simulation Integrity and Balance

1. **Defined rules — shipped.** Simulation modules, exact previews/receipts, and
   design records define each economic boundary.
2. **Predictable inputs — verified.** Seeded deterministic simulation and authored
   market schedule; uncertainty is explicit.
3. **Exploit/dominance tests — verified.** Balance profiles, invariant tests,
   strict transaction guards, persistence tamper tests, and bounded processing.
4. **No circular money — verified.** There is no resale loop; purchases are
   guarded one-way commitments and revenue only enters through filed outcomes.
5. **Upgrade balance — verified.** Capital, added recurring cost, prerequisites,
   risk, capacity, and opportunity cost are tested together.
6. **Scale testing — verified.** Opening, probation, mature campus, Senior,
   recovery, and runtime soak fixtures cover early through extreme authored scale.
7. **Large-value precision — shipped.** Integer cents and bounded authored totals;
   validators reject invalid ranges.
8. **Rounding/overflow/display — verified.** Half-up integer helpers, conservation
   receipts, strict saves, and display tests.
9. **Offline progress — scoped out.** The economy advances only while played, so
   system clocks cannot create idle income.
10. **Caps/diminishing/offline explanation — shipped.** Capacity, order limits,
    facility tiers, action limits, storage, and standing gates are disclosed;
    offline earnings do not exist.

## Saving and Reliability

1. **Complete economic save — shipped.** Cash, debt, inventory/lots, facilities,
   staffing, contracts/market terms, automation, events, progression, and flock.
2. **Autosave — shipped.** Checkpoints occur at major decisions, transactions,
   shift boundaries, and lifecycle events.
3. **Corruption/duplication/partial-write protection — verified.** Versioned strict
   schemas, validation, atomic restore, conservation receipts, and tamper tests.
4. **Backup/recovery — shipped.** Browser portable export/import and retriable
   campaign recovery.
5. **Offline timestamp protection — N/A.** No offline progress or clock-derived
   economy exists.
6. **Transfer/cloud — shipped where supported.** Portable backup is safe and
   explained; managed cloud sync is not part of this static single-player build.
7. **No silent loss — shipped.** Failed imports/restores reject atomically and
   preserve the prior state; transaction failures return reasons.

## Accessibility and Player Preferences

1. **Not color-only — shipped.** Labels, values, status words, reasons, patterns,
   and semantic narration accompany color.
2. **Color-vision indicators — shipped.** Configurable color-vision modes plus
   textual gain/loss/risk/opportunity states.
3. **Scalable information — shipped.** Text/interface scale and responsive layouts.
4. **Reduced sensory load — shipped.** Reduced motion, flashing/sensory effects,
   screen shake, and rapid-effect alternatives.
5. **Notification control — shipped.** Settings persist All Notices, Priority
   Only, or Shift Record Only for transient floor toasts. Every message remains
   in the bounded newest-first Shift Record with redundant ACTION, MILESTONE, or
   ROUTINE labels; modal decisions still require explicit acknowledgement, and
   five independent audio buses provide separate sound-layer muting.
6. **Input coverage — shipped.** Keyboard, mouse, controller, and touch.
7. **Reading control — shipped.** Pause and 1x/3x/10x.
8. **No forced rapid reaction — shipped.** Pause is always available; Priority
   Peck is optional and has non-rapid alternatives.

## Monetization and Fairness

All eight monetization requirements are **not applicable by design**. The build
contains no real-money purchases, premium currency, boosts, advertisements,
pay-to-win advantages, frustration sales, or purchase flow. The game is complete
with earned Feed Fund only, so there is no accidental real-money transaction to
confirm.
