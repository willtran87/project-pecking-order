# Cohesive Game Loop Advancement

This release resolves the approved twenty-five-item clarity, fun, depth, character, reward, and completion brief through the game's existing simulation and campaign authorities. It adds no competing panel, currency, save model, or command path.

## Immediate understanding

1. **Real first-player testing** — The existing local-only five-person protocol now sits in the release contract with first-route time, hesitation, misclick, icon recall, and explain-back measures. Evidence remains `AWAITING REAL PARTICIPANTS` until real sessions occur.
2. **Ruthless subtraction** — One primary action, at most two supporting metrics, zero required prose, hidden unavailable actions, and details on demand are enforced as a presentation budget.
3. **Icons taught through play** — Star/Best/Flow +1, Check/Safe/Flow Holds, and Triangle/Risky/Flow Resets follow `SHOW → TRY → REACT → EXPLAIN ON DEMAND` without a required legend.
4. **Clear first-shift story** — The measured cold open is `SAVE FILE → DISCOVER FIT → BUILD FLOW → CHANGE OFFICE`, with a protected payoff targeted inside 30 seconds.
5. **Persistent why inspection** — Holding `H` or activating the latest cause reconstructs Action, Target, Outcome, and one alternate path without mutating the simulation.

## Deeper decisions

6. **Flow cash-out** — The authoritative Active Playbook offers Bank the Clutch or Chase Premium with exact pace, shell-risk, and value consequences while preserving filed rewards.
7. **Contract drafting** — Shift plans and campaign challenge contracts disclose their stakes and remain reversible until filed.
8. **Visible bottlenecks** — Intake queues, occupied desks, strained hens, review, and filing remain physical, actionable office states rather than a spreadsheet-only problem.
9. **Pre-shift staffing** — Desk, lane, pair, break, and per-hen Auto Fit policy choices remain simulation-owned and world-visible.
10. **Predictable market conditions** — Authored scenarios disclose their pace, shell, value, quota, and rival effects; there is no hidden random punishment.
11. **Resource opportunity costs** — Feed Fund, favor, solidarity, compliance, and attention retain distinct uses and disclosed short/long-term tradeoffs.
12. **End-shift allocation** — Reward and recovery drafts preview a single filed destination across flock, equipment, standing, recovery, or office growth.

## Character and authored variety

13. **Multi-file case chains** — Hero-case choice history produces persistent callbacks and next-shift consequences.
14. **Hen dilemmas** — Personal objectives and hero choices put efficiency, care, credit, and deadlines into readable conflict with a valid recovery path.
15. **Relationship friction** — Bond episodes can disagree, mentor, cover, or specialize; relationships are not bonus-only modifiers.
16. **Rival boss shifts** — The visible rival race culminates in a Final Hearing that tests the player's filed build without a hidden counter.
17. **Signature content depth** — Eight hero files, seven deterministic file personalities, scenario identities, and shuffle-bag dialogue provide repeat-safe authored variety.
18. **Purposeful environment interactions** — Water cooler, coffee, whiteboard, printer, trophy shelf, and bulletin interactions support recovery, planning, events, rewards, and story callbacks.

## Reward, replay, and completion

19. **Exclusive upgrade branches** — Campaign milestone doctrines remain mutually exclusive, persistent, and strategy-defining.
20. **Physical transformations** — Upgrades and collection sockets change the office, equipment, routines, clients, and flock presentation.
21. **Strategy-specific endings** — Final evaluation and legacy evidence read office culture, strongest hen, rival relationship, client record, and doctrine.
22. **Distinct scenario modes** — Replay scenarios and challenge contracts change rules and objectives, not only starting numbers.
23. **Adaptive difficulty contracts** — Learning, Standard, and Expert disclose their pressure and safeguards while preserving the same core rules.
24. **Layered dynamic music** — Ambient office, shift pulse, pressure, scenario, and review layers follow Plan, Flow, Crisis, Recovery, and Reward states with non-audio equivalents.
25. **Real content cadence** — The target rhythm is a small payoff every 20–30 seconds, a strategic decision every 1–2 minutes, and a visible chapter transformation.

## Evidence boundary

Automated native and browser checks verify all twenty-five product mappings, their real gameplay authorities, presentation limits, and Web release integration. They do not impersonate human participants; the five-person evidence gate remains explicitly open.
