# Goal clarity and rewarding feedback

This pass addresses the twelve-point review by refining existing controls and
simulation-backed feedback. It adds no currencies, compulsory chores, artificial
successes, or retention-score claims.

## Changes

1. Personal goals use the actual report's `next_quota`, with compatible fallbacks.
   Loading repairs active legacy goals against restored quota authority; settling
   checks actual completed quota and output, so a saved quota of one cannot win.
2. On Web, bounded monotonic active time compensates for engine render-delta
   clamping. Suspended-tab gaps are discarded; pause and reading holds reset the
   baseline. Per-frame simulation work remains bounded to four ticks.
3. The menu stays **PLAN** at every presentation layer. Existing opportunity
   icons, tooltips, and menu entries disclose what's ready without renaming it.
4. Career badges stay out of first-egg induction; the immediate physical goal
   remains dominant. Career progress and necessary actions are retained.
5. Interactive desks and hen markers use the existing hover rail and cursor,
   plus a short action label. Clicking still opens a preview, not a purchase.
6. The optional check-in lesson highlights an available, affordable action,
   preferably the hen's signature. It offers Later, shows the actual before/after
   trust and stress after a filed action, and returns to work. Opening spends
   nothing; normal check-in costs and limits still apply.
7. Personal-goal progress retains clean-chain context. Delivery receipts identify
   the actual included clean bonus; no second payout is minted.
8. Goal offer, review plan preview, and a matching next-shift PLAN entry now share
   one recommendation. Previewing or accepting a goal never files a strategy.
9. Fast desks shuffle briskly, Safe desks pause between inspection strokes, and
   occupied working desks alone animate. Flock's existing lower-strain recovery
   remains authoritative. Reduced motion leaves the papers still.
10. Optional rhythm barks are spaced by eight seconds and suppressed during
    induction/modal decisions. Ambient chatter yields to these beats and incident
    presentation; required decisions are never deferred.
11. Real per-hen career egg totals recognize 10/25/50/100 eggs with a hen reaction
    and a named entry on the existing trophy shelf. Recognition reconstructs from
    saved worker history; it neither changes office furniture nor adds rewards.
12. Five unfamiliar human players remain required. The study card now explicitly
    covers the lesson, stable PLAN control, coherent goal, and named recognition.
    No sessions, enjoyment results, or normal-device timing results are fabricated.

## Evidence and limitations

Focused native regression covers actual report fields, legacy repair, truthful
settlement, bounded Web timing, goal persistence, equipment preview safety,
optional lesson cancellation/results, and stable PLAN naming. Browser audits use
real inputs; the review audit is explicitly an authored layout fixture.

The 2560×1600 software-rendered opening run reached the first collected egg in
47.562 seconds after routing (36.354 production, 11.208 presentation), compared
with the prior 183.488-second run. This is a substantial observed improvement,
not proof of the 30–45-second normal-device target or a controlled benchmark.
The native gate was running concurrently. Normal-device and human acceptance
remain separate from automated correctness.

Release verification passed 69 native checks, four web/toolchain checks, and all
six packaging checks after synchronizing the final export. The focused native
regression was rerun after final edits. Actual-input lesson and authored-review
audits passed at wide and compact sizes; the prescribed browser client also
passed at 1280×720. Test servers and browser resources were cleaned up.
